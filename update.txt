Project Deep Feature Plan (Business-Focused, Revenue-First)
Date: 2026-04-17

1) Current Product Snapshot (What is already strong)

- Multi-tenant baseline is in place:
  - Business, staff roles, customer ownership, service catalog, appointments, invoices.
- Public business pages and booking URLs exist:
  - Per-business slug landing and booking path.
- Core operational loop is working:
  - Service -> booking -> invoice -> mark paid.
- Mobile-style navigation is already introduced for public and dashboard views.
- Foundational security and quality controls exist:
  - User-scoped queries in key places, deploy checks clean, automated tests present.

This is a strong MVP foundation. The next step is not broad rewrites; it is strategic feature layering that grows revenue and retention.


2) Biggest Business Gaps (High-value opportunities)

Gap A: No automated reminder/recovery system
- Impact: no-show risk remains high.
- Revenue effect: direct loss from unused slots.

Gap B: No deposits / advance payments
- Impact: weak commitment from customers.
- Revenue effect: cancellations/no-shows hurt cash flow.

Gap C: No packages/memberships
- Impact: only one-time service billing.
- Revenue effect: low repeat predictability (LTV not maximized).

Gap D: Limited pricing intelligence
- Impact: static pricing for all times/days.
- Revenue effect: misses peak-hour margin opportunities.

Gap E: Weak rebooking and retention journeys
- Impact: after service completion, no auto rebook/follow-up funnel.
- Revenue effect: lower repeat bookings and customer lifetime value.

Gap F: Public page trust signals are still basic
- Impact: conversion friction for first-time visitors.
- Revenue effect: lower booking conversion rate from page traffic.


3) Recommended Feature Roadmap (Ordered by ROI)

Phase 1: Revenue Protection (Immediate)
Priority: Critical

Feature 1.1 - Advance Booking Deposit
- Add optional per-service deposit amount or deposit percentage.
- Capture status at booking (deposit_paid / deposit_pending).
- Block final confirmation or reserve slot for limited time if unpaid.
- Business value: fewer no-shows + better cash predictability.

Feature 1.2 - Cancellation and No-show Policy Engine
- Business-level settings:
  - free cancellation window (e.g., 12h)
  - no-show fee (fixed or percentage)
- Auto-apply policy on appointment state changes.
- Business value: protects revenue leakage.

Feature 1.3 - Reminder Automation Queue
- Schedule reminders at T-24h and T-2h.
- First channel: WhatsApp template-ready payload table (or SMS fallback).
- Track reminder_sent_at and delivery state.
- Business value: reduces no-shows immediately.


Phase 2: Conversion and Trust (Public Experience)
Priority: High

Feature 2.1 - Rich Public Profile Completeness Score
- Show business profile completion in dashboard:
  - hero, services, pricing, images, contact, map, testimonial.
- Prompt missing items with one-click actions.
- Business value: increases booking page conversion.

Feature 2.2 - Social Proof and Service FAQ Blocks
- Per-business testimonial list (not single quote only).
- FAQ block for policies, booking process, payment accepted.
- Business value: lowers hesitation, increases first-time booking confidence.

Feature 2.3 - Promo Banner / Offer Code on Public Page
- Time-bound offer per business (e.g., weekday 10% off).
- Show on landing and checkout summary.
- Business value: drives immediate campaign conversions.


Phase 3: Repeat Revenue Engine
Priority: High

Feature 3.1 - Packages
- Prepaid service bundles:
  - e.g., 5 sessions at discounted total.
- Track package balance and redemption history.
- Business value: upfront cash + repeat lock-in.

Feature 3.2 - Membership Plans
- Monthly membership with included services/discounts.
- Member pricing overrides standard catalog pricing.
- Business value: recurring predictable revenue.

Feature 3.3 - Rebook Flow
- “Book again” from last appointment with one tap.
- Suggested next date based on service cycle.
- Business value: boosts repeat rate.


Phase 4: Margin Optimization
Priority: Medium-High

Feature 4.1 - Dynamic Pricing Rules
- Peak hour/day multipliers.
- Staff/provider premium rules.
- Business value: higher yield during demand peaks.

Feature 4.2 - Add-on Upsell Engine
- Attach add-ons to services in booking flow.
- Show “frequently added” options at checkout.
- Business value: increases average order value (AOV).

Feature 4.3 - Revenue Analytics Upgrade
- Existing dashboard is good; add decision metrics:
  - conversion by source/page
  - repeat vs new revenue split
  - reminder impact on no-show rate
  - package/member revenue share.
- Business value: clearer growth decisions.


Phase 5: Ops Scale and Team Productivity
Priority: Medium

Feature 5.1 - Waitlist + Auto-fill
- If cancellation occurs, notify waitlist in order.
- Business value: recovers cancelled slot revenue.

Feature 5.2 - Staff Incentive Reports
- Revenue per provider, service mix, utilization.
- Business value: better staffing and incentives.

Feature 5.3 - Multi-location readiness
- Current model can evolve:
  - add Branch model and branch-scoped services/staff/hours.
- Business value: supports growth businesses without platform migration.


4) Suggested Data Model Expansions (Planning Only)

Core additions:
- Appointment:
  - source (web, walkin, whatsapp, phone)
  - cancellation_reason
  - reminder_status fields
- Billing:
  - payment_method
  - discount_amount
  - deposit_amount
- Customer:
  - last_visit_at
  - preferred_services
  - tags (VIP/new/at-risk)

New models to add in future iterations:
- ReminderLog
- DepositTransaction
- Package / PackagePurchase / PackageRedemption
- MembershipPlan / MembershipSubscription
- PricingRule
- OfferCampaign


5) What to Build Next (Exact order for maximum return)

If the goal is fastest business impact in next 2-4 weeks:

Week 1:
- Deposits + cancellation policy + reminder queue scaffolding.

Week 2:
- Public profile completeness + campaign banner + testimonial list.

Week 3:
- Packages + basic rebook flow.

Week 4:
- Dynamic pricing (simple rules) + upsells + expanded analytics.


6) Practical KPIs to Track After Rollout

Must-track metrics:
- Booking conversion rate (public page visits -> confirmed bookings)
- No-show rate
- Average order value
- Repeat booking rate (30 days)
- Revenue per day and per provider
- Deposit adoption rate
- Package/membership revenue share

Success definition:
- 20-30% no-show reduction
- 10-20% AOV increase
- 15%+ repeat booking lift


7) Final Recommendation

Do not over-expand feature surface all at once.
Focus first on:
1) no-show prevention (reminders + deposits),
2) repeat revenue (packages/rebook),
3) conversion trust (richer public business pages).

These three pillars will deliver the fastest and most measurable business gain from the current codebase.
